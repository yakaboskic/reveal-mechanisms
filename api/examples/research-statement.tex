\section*{Genes and gene programs in a shared CAD-in-T2D mechanism}
The selected CAD knowledge gap asks whether context amplifies genetic risk or instead reflects incipient disease.~\cite{dapper:KnowledgeGap.zNV20nhHamt-a4CeAktQQPoAivOJe6xk} In the captured CAD-in-T2D analysis, SHH, GLI3 and TCF7L2 are implicated in mechanism Factor1 by EAGGL loadings of 0.5042, 0.3245 and 0.3813, respectively.~\cite{dapper:Claim.TsqJgibQNjkHLCocO-AIKxiPUgi3o5km,dapper:Claim.Uv7_cT46DdsqZMvk5nh8G48qOIKvhRgc,dapper:Claim.I2mf6PJ0DPF5_nnbiBjAhgjlUCOiUZZ9} The AMP AD / GTEx brain-aging and PsychENCODE geneM16 programs are also implicated in this mechanism by their gene-set loadings.~\cite{dapper:Claim.Kd3psmdr4G9AlD0UU1E1Vu6AjpKaQYWE,dapper:Claim.NBafdtdEE2Uabs-8pZqurv7SoWpg7Yhi} PIGEAN gene--trait and gene-set--trait results further connect these genes and programs to CAD in people with type 2 diabetes.~\cite{dapper:Claim.Oe2UZeV7oMY4NsMHyB8h4HJO1FH4nSGF,dapper:Claim.2idJniYU4zCtCN6puy8XZ2mzo9Mq6c5p,dapper:Claim.H5icBYm5JLM3_T-b1mMfH65Nc0zMhZzH,dapper:Claim.xzV_35EYnLl-zy03YbmhfBk_ynNbl7H9,dapper:Claim.SYqCJXxCVXDpwflRSdZnkVLzGLO_vi_r} Illustrative, unverified KG membership assertions connect SHH to the AMP AD / GTEx set and TCF7L2 to the PsychENCODE set; these links demonstrate a possible evidence connection and are not retrieved biological findings.~\cite{dapper:Claim.x7aCVQ1uTkYKLJo32ZyAGMGZaAGCSvWV,dapper:Claim.uzqyfwuhf59hSTdravdcr4cTxuitWGx-} Taken together, these assessments prioritize a shared mechanism for investigating the gap, but do not resolve whether exposures amplify genetic risk or are downstream readouts. Temporal or intervention evidence is still needed.~\cite{dapper:KnowledgeGap.zNV20nhHamt-a4CeAktQQPoAivOJe6xk,dapper:Claim.TsqJgibQNjkHLCocO-AIKxiPUgi3o5km,dapper:Claim.Uv7_cT46DdsqZMvk5nh8G48qOIKvhRgc,dapper:Claim.I2mf6PJ0DPF5_nnbiBjAhgjlUCOiUZZ9,dapper:Claim.Kd3psmdr4G9AlD0UU1E1Vu6AjpKaQYWE,dapper:Claim.NBafdtdEE2Uabs-8pZqurv7SoWpg7Yhi}
