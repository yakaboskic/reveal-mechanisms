# Genes and gene programs in a shared CAD-in-T2D mechanism

> Authored design example; unpublished. CFDE observations are captured; KG membership assertions are illustrative and unverified.

The selected CAD knowledge gap asks whether context amplifies genetic risk or instead reflects incipient disease. [1](#reference-1) In the captured CAD-in-T2D analysis, SHH, GLI3 and TCF7L2 are implicated in mechanism Factor1 by EAGGL loadings of 0.5042, 0.3245 and 0.3813, respectively. [2](#reference-2)[3](#reference-3)[4](#reference-4) The AMP AD / GTEx brain-aging and PsychENCODE geneM16 programs are also implicated in this mechanism by their gene-set loadings. [5](#reference-5)[6](#reference-6) PIGEAN gene–trait and gene-set–trait results further connect these genes and programs to CAD in people with type 2 diabetes. [7](#reference-7)[8](#reference-8)[9](#reference-9)[10](#reference-10)[11](#reference-11) Illustrative, unverified KG membership assertions connect SHH to the AMP AD / GTEx set and TCF7L2 to the PsychENCODE set; these links demonstrate a possible evidence connection and are not retrieved biological findings. [12](#reference-12)[13](#reference-13) Taken together, these assessments prioritize a shared mechanism for investigating the gap, but do not resolve whether exposures amplify genetic risk or are downstream readouts. Temporal or intervention evidence is still needed. [1](#reference-1)[2](#reference-2)[3](#reference-3)[4](#reference-4)[5](#reference-5)[6](#reference-6)
